About Chetna Welfare Trust

Creating Better Tomorrow Through Education, Leadership & Community Development

Chetna Welfare Trust is dedicated to empowering underserved rural communities through equitable education, digital inclusion, youth leadership, sports-for-development and livelihood readiness initiatives. We believe every child and young person deserves access to opportunities that enable a brighter future.

Who We Are

Empowering Rural Communities Through Education & Opportunity

Chetna Welfare Trust is a non-profit organization working to strengthen underserved rural communities through education, digital inclusion, youth leadership and livelihood readiness initiatives.

We believe that every child and young person deserves access to quality learning, leadership opportunities and sustainable pathways for growth. Through community-driven programs and local partnerships, we aim to create lasting social impact.

Ground Realities

Challenges Faced By Rural Communities

Many children and young people in underserved communities continue to face barriers that limit their access to quality education, digital resources, leadership opportunities and sustainable livelihoods.

Learning Gaps

Many students struggle with foundational literacy and numeracy due to limited access to quality learning support.

Digital Divide

Rural communities often lack access to technology, digital tools and modern learning resources.

School Dropouts

Economic and social challenges force many children to discontinue their education at an early stage.

Limited Opportunities

Young people often lack career guidance, skill development and livelihood readiness support.

Community Participation

Strong collaboration between schools, families and communities is often missing.

Sustainable Development

Awareness regarding sustainability, energy conservation and environmental responsibility remains limited.

Our Solution

Building Opportunities Through Community Action

We work with children, youth, families and communities to create sustainable pathways for education, leadership, livelihood readiness and social development.

Sustainability & Awareness

Promoting environmental awareness, energy conservation and responsible community practices.
